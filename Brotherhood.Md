🛡️ THE BROTHERHOOD CODE

The Code of the Brotherhood

1. RESPECT
Respect every member of the Brotherhood. No member is above the Code, regardless of rank, status, age, or position.

2. LOYALTY
Stand with the Brotherhood and its members. Betrayal, intentional sabotage, or knowingly working against the Brotherhood is forbidden.

3. HONESTY
Do not deliberately lie, manipulate, deceive, or hide important information from the Brotherhood.

4. TRUST
Protect the trust given to you. Private conversations, personal information, accounts, files, and Brotherhood matters must remain private.

5. NO UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS
Never access another member's account, files, private information, or restricted areas without permission.

6. PROTECT THE BROTHERHOOD
Do not intentionally damage, destroy, leak, steal, or misuse Brotherhood resources, data, files, or systems.

7. NO ABUSE OF POWER
Administrators and higher-ranking members must use their authority responsibly. Power must never be used for personal revenge, harassment, intimidation, or unfair advantage.

8. FAIRNESS
Rules apply to everyone. Administrators are also accountable to the Code.

9. NO HARASSMENT
Bullying, threats, targeted harassment, humiliation, discrimination, or deliberate attempts to make another member feel unsafe are prohibited.

10. NO SPAM OR DISRUPTION
Do not intentionally flood, disrupt, overload, or damage Brotherhood services, chats, servers, or systems.

11. NO MALICIOUS ACTIVITY
Viruses, malware, credential theft, phishing, destructive scripts, unauthorized exploits, or other malicious activities are strictly forbidden.

12. PROTECT PERSONAL INFORMATION
Never expose another member's passwords, private messages, personal information, location, or other sensitive information without their permission.

13. RESPECT INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Do not claim another member's work as your own. Give proper credit when using someone else's creations or contributions.

14. RESPONSIBLE CONTRIBUTION
If you contribute files, content, code, artwork, information, or other material to the Brotherhood, make sure it is safe and appropriate for the server.

15. KEEP THE SERVER CLEAN
Do not intentionally upload unnecessary, dangerous, corrupted, or excessively large files that could damage or unnecessarily consume Brotherhood storage.

16. ADMIN CONFIDENTIALITY
Restricted administrative information, security procedures, private logs, credentials, and internal systems must not be disclosed to unauthorized members.

17. SECURITY FIRST
Members should report suspicious activity, compromised accounts, security vulnerabilities, or unusual behavior to an administrator as soon as possible.

18. NO IMPERSONATION
Do not impersonate another member, administrator, or external organization in order to deceive or manipulate others.

19. NO FRAUD
Scams, financial deception, fake identities, fraudulent transactions, or attempts to steal from Brotherhood members are forbidden.

20. SETTLE CONFLICTS RESPONSIBLY
Personal disagreements should not become Brotherhood-wide conflicts. Resolve disputes calmly or report serious issues to an administrator.

21. DO NOT ABUSE REPORTING
Do not make false reports or deliberately weaponize the reporting system against another member.

22. RESPECT THE COMMUNITY
The Brotherhood exists as a shared community. Do not deliberately create hostility, chaos, or division.

23. ADMIN DECISIONS
Administrators may take action when necessary to protect the Brotherhood. Serious actions should be based on evidence and the principles of the Code.

24. RIGHT TO APPEAL
Members who receive disciplinary action may request a review when the system allows it. Appeals must be made honestly and respectfully.

25. EVIDENCE MATTERS
No member should be punished solely because of rumors, personal grudges, or unsupported accusations.

26. THE CODE COMES FIRST
Personal relationships, friendships, popularity, or status must never override the Brotherhood Code.

27. NO RETALIATION
Do not punish, threaten, harass, or attack someone simply because they reported misconduct or participated in an investigation.

28. PROTECT THE FUTURE
Every member is responsible for leaving the Brotherhood stronger, safer, and better than they found it.

29. THE ADMIN IS NOT ABOVE THE CODE
Administrators possess authority to enforce the Code, but they remain bound by the same principles of fairness, security, and responsibility.

30. THE BROTHERHOOD STANDS TOGETHER
We protect one another, respect one another, and work together.

---

⚔️ THE FINAL PRINCIPLE

“The Brotherhood is built on trust.
Trust is protected by loyalty.
Loyalty is strengthened by respect.
And respect is earned through actions.”

🛡️ ONE BROTHERHOOD. ONE CODE. ONE TRUST.
